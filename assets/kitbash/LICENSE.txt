Real-world guns, kitbashed. Each gun here is built by a script in art-src/guns/ (zed_guns.py has one recipe per gun) from parts of the CC0 sources below, cut, reshaped and joined with parts modelled in the script, recoloured to this pack's own palette (zed_kit.py), bevelled, and exported as glTF (muzzle on +Z, origin at the middle of the base). No gun is a copy of any one source model. Rebuild: blender -b --python art-src/guns/build.py

Quaternius, "Ultimate Low Poly Guns Pack" (July 2019)
  https://opengameart.org/content/low-poly-guns-pack  (also https://quaternius.com)
  Licence: CC0 1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ (stated on the page; the download carries no licence file)
  Parts taken, per gun (everything else on each gun is modelled in the script):
    service_pistol      Pistol_3 frame and slide
    silenced_pistol     Pistol_6 frame and slide; Silencer_2 accessory (shrunk)
    uzi                 SubmachineGun_3 receiver, grip and magazine (foregrip removed, lengthened)
    tactical_smg        SubmachineGun_5 receiver and stock; Silencer_2, Scope_3 and Flashlight accessories
    pump_shotgun        Shotgun_2 receiver and stock
    sawed_off           Shotgun_ShortStock receiver and pistol-grip stock (barrels removed)
    hunting_rifle       SniperRifle_6 stock and action (scope removed); Scope_1 accessory
    machine_gun         AssaultRifle_1 receiver; AssaultRifle_5 stock
    grenade_launcher    Revolver_2 frame and grip; Stock and Grip accessories
    rocket_launcher     Grip accessory (twice); Scope_2 accessory
    flamethrower        Grip accessory (twice)
  Removed from the donors and replaced: barrels, handguards, muzzle devices, sights, scopes, foregrips.

Kenney, "Blaster Kit" 2.1
  https://kenney.nl/assets/blaster-kit
  Licence: CC0 1.0
  Parts taken: scope-small (the silenced pistol's red dot).

Modelled in the scripts (CC0 with the rest of this folder's art): barrels, tubes, muzzle devices, sights, rails, rings, bipod legs, tanks, nozzles and the other parts the recipes build from boxes and cylinders.
