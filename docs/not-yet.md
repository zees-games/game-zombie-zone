# Not yet

What this game does not do yet, each with where the detail is.

- **Workshop maps are listed, not playable.** `provides.json` accepts subscribed maps of `map_kind` `survival` and `exodus`; the solo map menu (M) and the solo ballot list them as `Name [Pack]` (`game/zz_workshop.gd`), and choosing one answers "Zombie Zone cannot play Workshop maps yet." Online lists none (the content index answers for its session). A pack's map is a scene, but this game builds every map from a document (`ZzMapDoc`: solid boxes, props, spawns, safe areas, route, nav floors, items, objectives) through `ZzCatalogue` and `ZzMap`, and the horde's nav graph, the safe rooms and the survival waves are all cut from that document's floor plan. Playing a pack map needs a document derived from the scene (boxes from its collision shapes, spawn and safe-area markers, a floor plan for the nav graph) or a pack-supplied document next to the scene, and `ZzCatalogue` taking it by entry key.
- **Weapons are not accepted.** Every weapon comes from `ZzWeapons.all_defs()` (zee-dot-weapons, and zed when `second_weapon_pack` is on), by tier tags, and the loadout, boxes, shop and view models read that fixed list. Following `DotWeaponCatalogue.follow_content` needs `ZzWeapons` to read a catalogue instead of the pack list, and a Workshop weapon needs a tier tag and a view model the box tables can use.
